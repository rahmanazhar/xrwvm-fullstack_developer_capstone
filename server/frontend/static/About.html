About Us

Best Cars Dealerships is a nationwide network of 50 independently owned showrooms, connected by one simple idea: buying a car should be as transparent as reading a review of it. Every dealership on this site is rated by the people who actually bought from it, and every review is published unedited — positive, neutral or negative.

We started in 2019 with three showrooms in Texas. Today we help more than 40,000 customers a year compare dealers, read verified reviews and walk in already knowing what to expect.
